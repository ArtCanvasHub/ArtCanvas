My Bio

Digital artist specializing in sci-fi environments and fantasy character illustration. I explore the intersection of organic shapes and technological aesthetics — building worlds that feel both alien and strangely familiar. Every piece starts with a question: what would this place feel like to stand in?

I've been painting digitally for over a decade, starting with fantasy concept art before moving into hard sci-fi environments. My work has been featured in several indie game projects and album covers.
